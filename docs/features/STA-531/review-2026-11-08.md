## Framing audit (2026-11-08, condensed)

Patterns across all six pages, to remove or reframe:
1. "No till/integrations" and "X répond à ce besoin, Stampeo non", repeated in intros, strengths, FAQs and switching sections.
2. "by hand" / « à la main » (Stampeo's till row in four strings, every switching section).
3. "can't import" / « ne permet pas d'ajouter un client à la main », in every switching section. Now: free import with our team.
4. "yet" / « pas encore » (offline, geolocation).
5. "optional levels" / « niveaux en option » (stampeo.ts in four strings, Zerosix).
6. "Starter has no broadcasts, but…", on five pages.
7. "Starter has 2 team members (you and one more)". The unlimited team on Growth/Pro is never said.
8. "more than one location needs Pro".
9. « n'est pas pensé pour les chaînes ni les franchises ».
10. The free card design shown as a fallback inside the competitor's strengths (« si vous manquez de temps »).
11. All three FR pages open « X convient mieux que Stampeo »; switching sections start « gardez X : Stampeo ne fait ni l'un ni l'autre ».
12. The « Quatre langues » block copied on all FR pages; languages are a Choose-Stampeo reason on Loopy and Fidelatoo.
13. SMS named without its cost in intros, Choose-if, strengths and Fit (Stamp Me, Fidelatoo, Zerosix, Hey Pongo).
14. A self-hedged export ("not their stamps", "sign-up details only").
15. Never mentioned anywhere: analytics, team, set up in minutes, and that Stampeo costs less where it does (Stamp Me at every tier, Fidelatoo, Zerosix, Loopy's Growth and Ultimate vs our Growth and Pro).

Competitor points framed as strengths that a small shop doesn't need:
- **Loopy:** Zapier/Make/API (×5), geo-fencing.
- **Stamp Me:**
  - their own app as the headline;
  - in-app discovery, which only exists because customers install their app;
  - StampPod hardware on $79+ plans;
  - games and Birthday Club;
  - an account manager on the $199 plan.
- **WinStamp:** "one app for many shops", chains up to 20 shops, extra program options.
- **Fidelatoo:** sign-up by phone number at the till, a shopping-street shared card, Google reviews/CRM.
- **Zerosix:** 70 integrations and e-commerce, franchises, a dedicated expert with a 190 € HT setup fee, paid add-on modules.
- **Hey Pongo:**
  - 60+ integrations and kiosks;
  - Google reviews and listings;
  - franchises;
  - a demo and quote presented as a plus;
  - "operational in 48 hours" (Stampeo is live the same day).

Each "Choose X if" also lists things Stampeo does:
- remote stamping (from the dashboard, and the web scanner);
- several locations (Pro);
- location-filtered messages (Pro);
- an expert to set up (free card design).
