## Who they're for
From the site: the program suits "any type of business looking to incentivise and reward repeat behaviour", with examples such as cafes, salons, retail stores and universities, and "Businesses with highly repeatable products or services" (https://www.stampme.com/how-it-works, 2026-11-04). The pricing page adds: "We work with franchises, councils/governments, not-for-profits, large brands and more." The product is built around a customer app, optional merchant-side stamping hardware (StampPod, StampTag) and messaging tools (push, SMS, games). Its pages do not describe a card that works without the Stamp Me app or web app.

## Product shape
- The card lives in the Stamp Me app (free, no in-app purchases). Customers download the app, create an account "by registering a few basic personal details" and join. Customers can also join through the "Stamp Me web app" and keep using the card in a browser.
- Apple Wallet / Google Wallet: when the business uses the "Stamp Business" app, the customer can save their Member Code to the wallet and show it at checkout. The full loyalty experience stays in the Stamp Me app. On Trustpilot the company says Apple and Google wallet capabilities are in development (see Open questions).
- Staff stamping options: StampPod (countertop tap-and-go pod), StampTag (tap tag), OneStamps (single-use codes on products or packaging), Stamp Code, Stamp Business app (scans the Member Code), Merchant Console / Loyalty Portal (web).
- Management: web "Loyalty Portal" / "Merchant Console".

## Where they're stronger than Stampeo
- Stamping options beyond a phone: StampPod and StampTag tap devices, OneStamps codes on products or packaging, a Stamp Code, and the web Loyalty Portal (`staff.scanning`, `staff.pod`). Stampeo stamps by scanning in its Scanner app, or by hand from a customer's profile in the dashboard (STAMPEO.md §5, §6).
- Messaging tools: SMS on Pro and Elite at a per-message charge (`sms`), Scratch & Win games, Birthday Club and Lapsed Customers (`games`), and reward-expiry reminders (`push.automatic`). Stampeo sends no SMS, has no birthday automation or games, and sends no reward-expiry reminders (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Push to a single member, or to members filtered by location and Stamp Card (`push.targeting`). Stampeo's broadcasts start on Growth and target groups, never a single customer (STAMPEO.md §6 Broadcasts; the production broadcast filters in `app/services/notifications/segmentation.py` have no per-customer key).
- A browser option for customers (`customer.web_app`) and in-app discovery within 100 km (`discovery.in_app`).
- Zapier integrations to mailing and CRM tools (`integrations`; per-plan inclusion unreadable). Stampeo has no integrations or public API (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Phone support appears on the pricing page (`support`; plan not readable). Stampeo support is email, priority email on Pro (STAMPEO.md §7; features.py `general.support_level`).
- Track record and scale claimed by the vendor: "15+ years", 12.4M stamps issued, 5.0 App Store rating on 2.5K ratings (`size.stamps`, `size.app_rating.customer`).
- Bringing an existing list: bulk import by request and email invitations, up to 500 per upload (`customers.import`, `customers.invite`). Stampeo cannot add customers by hand (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Unlimited users in every plan's list (`team.users`), against 2 people on Stampeo Starter; Stampeo Growth and Pro have no team cap (STAMPEO.md §5).
- More languages for the customer app, per the site's claim (`languages`; the US App Store lists English only).

## Where Stampeo is stronger
- No app or account for the customer: the card is an Apple Wallet or Google Wallet pass that updates itself and notifies on the lock screen (STAMPEO.md §1, §2, §6 Automatic notifications). Stamp Me's wallet option holds a Member Code and needs the app and an account first (`card.where`, `customer.app_required`).
- Joining is a QR scan, a short sign-up form whose fields the business chooses (name and email by default, or none), and one tap to add the card (STAMPEO.md §4).
- Loyalty levels (up to 5, with card looks, perks and gifts) on every plan, basket boosters on points programs (Growth and Pro), and a guided stamps-to-points switch that keeps customers' cards (STAMPEO.md §6). Stamp Me's pages do not state levels or boosters (`program.types`).
- A notification on every scan and reward, plus milestone messages on Growth (3) and Pro (unlimited), delivered through the wallet pass on Apple and Google (STAMPEO.md §6). Stamp Me does not state a notification on each stamp (`push.automatic`).
- A wallet-card designer with a live Apple and Google Wallet preview and per-language card text (STAMPEO.md §6 Card designer).
- Dashboard, Scanner, card and messages in English, French, Spanish and Polish (STAMPEO.md §1).
- Multi-location on Pro with no cap and no charge per location, with per-location stats, QR codes and staff (STAMPEO.md §6 Multi-location). Stamp Me's pricing page lists a single location on every plan, while other pages describe multiple sites (`locations.multiple`, unresolved).

## Our gaps that matter here
From STAMPEO.md §8:
- No POS or integration of any kind, and no public API (Stamp Me offers Zapier).
- No offline scanning yet (coming soon): the Scanner needs a connection.
- No SMS or email marketing to customers; wallet notifications only.
- No gift cards, birthday automation, review prompts or referrals.
- No reward-expiry reminders.
- No adding customers by hand or bulk import.
- No tap hardware: stamping is the Scanner app reading the wallet QR, or a manual add from the dashboard.
- No geofencing yet (coming soon).
- Email support only (priority email on Pro); no enterprise tier or SLA.

- **Choose Stamp Me if:**
  - You want a customer app, with a browser option, where customers can find your shop and play Scratch & Win.
  - You want tap-to-stamp hardware (StampPod, StampTag) or single-use OneStamps codes on products.
  - You want SMS alongside push, messages to one customer, and Zapier hooks to your mailing list.
  - You have a customer list to bring over and want it imported or invited by email.
- **Choose Stampeo if:**
  - You want customers to add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with no app and no account.
  - You want loyalty levels, or stamps or points with a lock-screen notification on every scan from the wallet card itself.
  - You want to design the card with a live wallet preview, and run it in English, French, Spanish or Polish.
- **Two-sentence verdict:** Stamp Me and Stampeo both replace paper punch cards, but Stamp Me is built around its own customer app, optional tap hardware and messaging tools with push, SMS and games, while Stampeo puts the card straight into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with no app to download. Pick Stamp Me if you want that app, hardware and SMS; pick Stampeo if you want customers to join with one QR scan and keep the card in the wallet they already use.

## Switching from Stamp Me
Customers re-join by scanning Stampeo's new QR code and adding the card to their wallet; stamps and points do not import automatically, so the shop decides how to honor existing balances (for example, with the welcome gift head start, which STAMPEO.md §6 offers on every plan). Stamp Me members keep the old app until they delete it. The shop can download its member list from the Loyalty Portal (`export`), but Stampeo cannot add customers by hand, so customers join themselves. Stamp Me has no lock-in contract (`commitment`). The Stampeo card can be live the same day.
