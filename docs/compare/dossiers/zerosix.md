## Who they're for
Restaurants (traditional, fast food, dark kitchens, bars, cafés), food trades (bakeries, wine shops, grocers, butchers), retailers (fashion, vape, CBD, jewellery), independent opticians ("Premier acteur fidélité pour les opticiens indépendants"), and multi-site businesses and franchises ("Une solution également pensée pour les multi-sites et franchises"). Homepage: "Simplifiez et automatisez la fidélisation de vos clients avec notre solution marketing..." Their pages describe earning through a till (POS) integration, with a back-office for shops whose till is not integrated.

## Product shape
- The card is the customer's "espace fidélité", a web page opened from the phone, with an Apple/Google Wallet pass the customer can identify with. No app download: "Vos membres n'ont pas besoin de télécharger une énième application."
- Enrolment: at the till or any order channel (staff enter the customer's mobile number, the customer gets an SMS to finish signing up), or alone via QR code, referral, a game or social networks and the website.
- Earning: automatic when the till software is integrated (70+ integrations claimed). Without integration, a back-office ("Identifiez vos clients sur notre backoffice") where staff enter the customer's phone number, or the customer scans a QR code to show their profile. How staff then add points manually is not described on the pages read; no staff app or tablet is mentioned.
- Management: web back-office (pro.zerosix.com), statistics tables, weekly reports, SMS campaigns and automations, referral, boosters, review booster. A dedicated expert for the first meeting, configuration and staff training. Data hosted on AWS in Europe (Paris and Madrid data centres).

## Where they're stronger than Stampeo
- Till integration: 70+ integrations claimed, points added from the till with no extra staff action (pos.integration, staff.scanning). Stampeo has no POS integration (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Spend-based earning tied to the till, with product or category boosters (program.types).
- More programme types: cagnotte (money pot), club, reward shop, plus vouchers in euro or percent (program.types, program.rewards). Stampeo has stamps or points, with optional levels (§6).
- SMS campaigns with birthday, postcode, home-establishment, visit-frequency and last-visit filters, in the offer (SMS units extra) (push.broadcasts, sms.price). Stampeo sends no SMS or email to customers and has no birthday or custom-answer targeting (§6, §8).
- Geolocation notification near the shop (push.geo); Stampeo's geofencing is coming soon, not live (§8).
- Franchises and groups: one programme, franchisee-only statistics, local campaigns from head-office templates (locations.multiple).
- Founded 2014, +5,000 clients claimed, a dedicated expert for setup and staff training, takeover of an existing programme (company.founded, size.merchants, onboarding).
- Automated Google review requests (extra.google_reviews). Stampeo has no review prompts (§8).
- Referral and a prize-wheel game as add-on modules (program.extras).
- Spanish version of the site and a Spanish client base (languages, 2023 press).

## Where Stampeo is stronger
- The wallet pass is the card: the customer scans a QR code and adds it to Apple or Google Wallet, with no web loyalty space and no SMS confirmation step, and no account (STAMPEO.md §1, §4). Zerosix's card is a web loyalty space with a wallet pass, and till enrolment finishes by SMS (card.where, customer.signup).
- Staff scan the customer's wallet pass with the Stampeo Scanner app on a phone (iOS and Android), with no till, back-office or phone-number typing (§5). Zerosix's pages mention no staff app (staff.scanning).
- Self-service card designer with live Apple/Google Wallet preview on every plan (§6). Zerosix's pages do not say who designs the wallet card (designer).
- A guided switch between stamps and points that keeps customers' cards, on every plan (§6). Switching is not described on Zerosix's pages.
- A free trial (30 days in France per STAMPEO.md §7, rendered from the live catalog) and a real test card on the owner's phone before paying (§9). Zerosix states no trial (trial).
- One Pro plan covers any number of locations with no extra charge per location (§6 Multi-location). Zerosix prices per establishment (price.entry).
- Dashboard, Scanner, cards and notifications in French, English, Spanish and Polish (§1). Zerosix's site is in French and Spanish (languages).

## Our gaps that matter here

- No till (POS) integration: stamping or entering a spend is manual in the Scanner.
- Scanning needs a connection; offline scanning is coming soon (§5, §8).

- No SMS or email to customers; everything goes through the wallet pass.
- No targeting by spend, birthday or custom answers (§6).
- No money-pot or club programme type; one programme per business (§6).
- One card design per business; no per-location design.
- No review prompts, no referral mechanics for customers.

## Draft verdict (for Harry's gate)
- **Choose Zerosix if:**
  - Your till or online shop is among its integrations and you want points added automatically at payment.
  - You want SMS campaigns filtered by birthday, postcode, visit frequency or last visit.
  - You run several sites or a franchise and want one programme with local campaigns and per-site statistics.
  - You want a dedicated expert to set up the programme and train staff, and accept a setup fee.
- **Choose Stampeo if:**
  - You want customers to add the card to Apple or Google Wallet from a QR code, with no SMS step, and staff to stamp with a phone.
  - You have no till to connect, or do not want one, and a scan at the counter is enough.
  - You want to design the card yourself and test it on your own phone during a free trial.
  - You run several shops and want them on one Pro plan with no charge per location.
- **Two-sentence verdict:** Zerosix suits a shop that wants its loyalty points to follow the till automatically, with SMS campaigns and a team to set it up, priced per establishment with a setup fee. Stampeo suits an independent shop that wants a wallet card its customers add in seconds and its staff stamp with a phone, with no till integration to connect.

## Switching from Zerosix
Customers re-join by scanning a new Stampeo QR code and adding a new wallet card. Balances do not import automatically (STAMPEO.md describes no import; adding customers by hand is not available). Zerosix terms: end the contract with one month notice by email (CGV art. 7); the merchant's data is returned at contract end (within 30 working days at most per the CGV). A shop would run both in parallel during the notice period. Nothing else verified.
