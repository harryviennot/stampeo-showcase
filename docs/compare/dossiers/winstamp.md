## Who they're for
Homepage: "The loyalty app that helps your business to grow". Pricing is by number of locations (1, up to 5, up to 20, a quote beyond), so the pages address single shops through small multi-location businesses. The pages describe a card held in the WinStamp customer app, which collects programs from many businesses; they do not describe an Apple Wallet or Google Wallet card.

## Product shape
- Card location: inside the WinStamp customer app (iOS and Android), which holds the loyalty programs of several businesses. No WinStamp page describes a pass in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet; the iOS customer listing's "Supports" section shows Wallet, with Apple's standard line and no explanation (`card.where`).
- The customer installs the app and creates a free account, gets a personal QR code, and shows it to collect stamps (customer app listing). The business listing says "No registration information requested from your customers."
- Staff stamp in the WinStamp Business app: "Stamps are only added through the business app." "No extra devices or hardware needed - just your phone."
- Business management: the Business app (iOS and Android). Features listed in the description and version notes: customizable stamp cards (count, color, stamp image), customer management, bulk messaging, live stats, welcome reward, birthday rewards, validity periods, multiple loyalty programs per business, customer feedback, removal of stamps and points given by mistake.
- Billing is through Apple or Google in-app purchase, not on the website.

## Where they're stronger than Stampeo
- Entry price: Basic is $9.99 a month and includes unlimited customers, stampings, employees and devices (`price.entry`, `customers.cap`, `team.users`). Stampeo price: from the live catalog.
- Multi-location below the top plan: up to 5 locations on Plus and up to 20 on Premium, with a quote beyond that (`locations.multiple`). In Stampeo, multi-location is on Pro only, with no cap (STAMPEO.md §6).
- 20 app languages, including Finnish, German, Italian and Japanese (`languages`); Stampeo covers French, English, Spanish and Polish (STAMPEO.md §1).
- A customer app that gathers cards from many businesses and has 4.1K ratings at 4.9, so customers who already use WinStamp at other shops have the app installed (`card.where`, `size.app_rating.customer`).
- Birthday rewards (`rewards.extras`); Stampeo has no automated birthday rewards (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Several loyalty programs per business (`program.multiple`); Stampeo runs one program per business (STAMPEO.md §6).
- Customer feedback collected in the app (`feedback`); not in STAMPEO.md.
- Stamps tracked by date and by employee in Statistics on every plan (`stats.staff`). Stampeo names who made each scan in the Activity feed on every plan, and shows each member's last scan and all-time scans on Growth and Pro (STAMPEO.md §5).
- Billing goes through Apple or Google in-app purchase, with receipts and refunds from them (`billing.channel`, `refunds`).

## Where Stampeo is stronger
- Customers do not install an app or create an account: the card is added from a web page to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet (STAMPEO.md §1, §4), versus a customer app with a free account (`card.where`, `customer.app_required`).
- A web dashboard and a card designer with a live Apple and Google Wallet preview (STAMPEO.md §6 Card designer); WinStamp's management is in the mobile Business app as far as its pages show (`designer`).
- Points programs with a reward menu and up to 5 loyalty levels on every plan, plus basket boosters on Growth and Pro (STAMPEO.md §6), versus stamp cards with levels not stated (`program.types`).
- A notification on the wallet pass for every scan and reward, plus milestone messages on Growth and Pro, on Apple and Google (STAMPEO.md §6), versus not stated for WinStamp (`push.automatic`).
- Customer export as a file, owner only, on every plan (STAMPEO.md §6 Managing customers), versus not stated (`export`).

## Draft verdict (for Harry's gate)
- **Choose WinStamp if:**
  - You have up to 20 locations and want a plan priced by location count, and your customers are happy to use a loyalty app.
  - Your customers already use the WinStamp app, or you need an app language Stampeo does not offer.
  - You want several loyalty programs in one business, or birthday rewards.
  - You prefer to pay through your Apple or Google account.
- **Choose Stampeo if:**
  - You want customers to add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet from a QR code, with no app and no account.
  - You want points with a reward menu, loyalty levels, or a wallet notification on every scan.
  - You want to manage the program from a web dashboard with a live wallet-card preview, and export your customer list.
- **Two-sentence verdict:** WinStamp and Stampeo both give independent shops a digital stamp card, but WinStamp's card lives in a customer app while Stampeo's lives in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet with nothing to install. WinStamp suits shops that want pricing by location count up to 20 locations, several programs and its app's reach; Stampeo suits shops that want a no-app card, points, levels and wallet notifications. Stampeo price: from the live catalog.

## Switching from WinStamp
Customers re-join by scanning a Stampeo QR code; balances do not import automatically, since STAMPEO.md does not describe an import, and Stampeo cannot add customers by hand. Nothing on WinStamp's pages describes a business data export (`export`: Not stated), so the owner should ask WinStamp support what they can get. The WinStamp subscription is managed through Apple or Google in-app purchase (`commitment`, `billing.channel`). Running both for a few weeks while customers move over is the practical route; Stampeo cards can be live the same day.
