- Company: HQ Paris (Maddyness). Launched 2017 by Ludwig Jamet and Nicolas Samir, joined by Nora Barault (FrenchWeb, 14/02/2023). Legal entity name not checked.
- Markets and languages: French site; no other product language on the pages read. FrenchWeb (14/02/2023) says the 2023 funds were for growth in Europe, starting with European countries, without naming them.   - Staff: 55 (FrenchWeb company profile, 2023).
  - Independent ratings: none found. Appvizer: 0 reviews. Shopify app: "Note 0,0 (0 Avis)". No Trustpilot, Capterra or G2 page found; TrustRadius returned 403 to the researcher (not re-opened).

## Who they're for
Restaurants and food shops, from independents to chains: the homepage addresses "À tous les restaurateurs et commerces de bouche qui veulent fidéliser, collecter des avis, lancer des campagnes et piloter leur marketing local, sans multiplier les outils." It has separate pages for independents, brands and franchises. It is a marketing platform (loyalty, Google reviews, local listings, SMS/email/push campaigns), not only a loyalty card. Its homepage addresses restaurants and food shops; its blog also has pages for hairdressers.

## Product shape
- Card: customers join through one of six channels listed on the loyalty page: a counter tablet, an ordering kiosk, the card terminal at payment, a table QR code, Apple or Google Wallet, or the mobile app. The independents page says customers type their number at payment and that the card is on their phone.
- Customer app: the homepage offers "une vraie brand app Pongo à vos couleurs"; the loyalty page lists the app as one channel and does not say it is required.
- Staff: the pages read do not describe how staff credit points. The independents page says Pongo connects to the existing till ("Vous gardez votre caisse et vos habitudes : Pongo s'y connecte."). Integration counts differ by page: "plus de 40 partenaires" and "+ 80 intégrations" (homepage), "plus de 60 solutions" (integrations page).
- Business side: a dashboard; a Zendesk help centre exists (heypongohelp.zendesk.com, HTTP 403 to WebFetch).
- Setup: "La plupart des indépendants sont opérationnels en 48 heures." and "Notre équipe vous accompagne à chaque étape." (independents page).

## Where they're stronger than Stampeo
- Till integration: tills, kiosks, card terminals and click-and-collect tools are connected so enrolment can happen at payment (pos.integration, customer.signup). Stampeo has no POS integration (STAMPEO.md §8).
- Several enrolment channels: counter tablet, kiosk, card terminal, table QR code, wallet, app (card.where). Stampeo customers join from a QR code or link to the sign-up page and add the card to their wallet (§4).
- Marketing breadth: SMS and email as well as push, automated triggers (birthday, inactivity, points threshold) (push.automatic, push.broadcasts). Stampeo sends no SMS or email to customers and has no birthday automation (§8).
- Google reviews and local listings in the same tool: automatic review requests, replies, listings synced on 20+ platforms (extra.google_reviews, extra.local_listings). Stampeo has no review prompts (§8).
- Chains and franchises: a multi-site offer with a consolidated dashboard (locations.multiple). Stampeo has no enterprise tier and is not built for chains or franchises (§1, §8).
- Reward variety: experiential rewards, free products, a points-per-euro ratio, points for interactions beyond purchases (program.types).
- 4,000+ venues claimed, 5M euros raised in 2023, 55 staff in 2023 (size.merchants, size.funding, size.staff).
- Accompanied setup: a demo, a 48-hour launch claim for independents, and a team that supports each step (onboarding, trial).

## Where Stampeo is stronger
- Self-serve with prices on the website: Hey Pongo publishes no prices and starts with a demo (price.entry, trial). Stampeo's three plans, including Pro, are self-serve, monthly or yearly, with a free trial (STAMPEO.md §7; prices and trial length from the live catalog).
- The card lives in Apple or Google Wallet and nothing else is needed: no customer app, no account (§1, §8). On Hey Pongo the wallet pass is one of six channels and a branded app is part of the offer (card.where, designer).
- Lock-screen notifications through the wallet pass on Apple and Google, on every scan and reward (§6). Hey Pongo's automated triggers are described as SMS or email (push.automatic); a push campaign module is listed, and whether push goes through the wallet pass is not stated.
- Built for independent shops of any kind: cafés, bakeries, salons, market stalls (§1). Hey Pongo's homepage addresses restaurants and food shops (Who they're for).
- No caps on customers or scans on any plan (§8). logiciels.pro says Hey Pongo's offers vary by usage quotas (commitment; third party).
- Dashboard, Scanner, card and notifications in French, English, Spanish and Polish (§1). Hey Pongo's site is in French only (languages).

## Our gaps that matter here
From STAMPEO.md §8:
- No POS integration: staff add stamps or points by hand in the Scanner app.
- No marketing SMS or email to customers: wallet notifications only.
- No review prompts, no birthday automation, no gift cards.
- No offline scanning yet (the Scanner needs a connection); no geofencing yet.
- No enterprise tier and no public API; not built for chains or franchises.
- No permanent free tier (logiciels.pro marks no free version for Hey Pongo either; its Shopify app is free).

## Draft verdict (for Harry's gate)
- **Choose Hey Pongo if:**
  - You run a restaurant or food shop and want loyalty tied to your till, kiosk or ordering tool so customers join at payment.
  - You want Google review requests, local listings and SMS or email campaigns in the same tool as your loyalty programme.
  - You run a multi-site network or franchise and want one dashboard across sites.
  - You prefer a demo and a quote built for your business, with a team that sets things up with you.
- **Choose Stampeo if:**
  - You want the card in Apple or Google Wallet with nothing for customers to download, at a price you can read before talking to anyone.
  - You want to set up the card yourself and try it on your own phone before paying.
  - You prefer lock-screen notifications from the wallet card to SMS and email, and have no till integration need.
  - You run a shop outside restaurants and food, such as a salon or a market stall.
- **Two-sentence verdict:** Hey Pongo is a marketing platform for restaurants and food shops that combines loyalty with till integrations, Google reviews and SMS or email campaigns, priced by quote after a demo. Stampeo is a loyalty card that lives in Apple or Google Wallet with nothing for customers to download, set up by the owner with prices on the website, for shops that want loyalty without till integration or SMS.

## Switching from Hey Pongo
Customers would join through Stampeo's QR code and install a new wallet card; balances do not import automatically (STAMPEO.md has no import feature). The owner builds the card in the designer and prints the flyer. Hey Pongo's export options and contract terms were not found, so say nothing about leaving it. Stampeo has no till integration, so a shop that enrols through its till today would move to the Scanner app.
