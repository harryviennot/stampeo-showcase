## Where Stampeo is stronger

<Strengths side="stampeo">

**Nothing to install.** A Stampeo customer scans your QR code and adds the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in seconds, with no app and no account. WinStamp customers install its app and show its QR code at the counter. Your staff need no app either: they can scan from any phone or tablet browser at scan.stampeo.app, or use the Scanner app.

**A notification on every scan.** The Stampeo card updates itself and shows each stamp, reward and level-up on the lock screen, on Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. WinStamp doesn't publish automatic notifications.
