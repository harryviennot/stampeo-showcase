WinStamp keeps the stamp card in its own customer app and costs less than Stampeo, with plans priced by how many locations you run. Stampeo puts the card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with nothing to install, and offers points, loyalty levels and a lock-screen notification on every stamp.

<ChooseIf side="competitor">

- You want the lower monthly price, with plans set by your number of locations.
- You run two to five shops and want them on the middle plan, not the top one.
- Your customers already collect WinStamp cards from other shops in its app.
- You want several programs, birthday rewards, or an app language Stampeo doesn't offer.

</ChooseIf>

<ChooseIf side="stampeo">

- You want customers to add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, with nothing to install.
- You want a lock-screen notification on every stamp and reward.
- You want loyalty levels, or points with a reward menu, on any plan.
- You want to download your customer list as a file, on any plan.

</ChooseIf>

## Where WinStamp is stronger

<Strengths side="competitor">

**Price, with no caps.** WinStamp is the lower-cost option of the two, and each plan includes <Fact id="customers.cap" /> plus <Fact id="team.users" />. Stampeo doesn't cap customers or scans either, but its Starter has <Fact id="stampeo.team.starter" /> team members (you and one more).

**One app for many shops.** The WinStamp app holds cards from many businesses, so customers who already use it elsewhere add yours in the same place.

**More languages.** WinStamp offers <Fact id="languages" />. Stampeo's card, Scanner app and dashboard come in four: English, Spanish, French and Polish.

**Program extras.** WinStamp lets you run <Fact id="program.multiple" />. Its Business app adds <Fact id="rewards.extras" />, plus <Fact id="feedback" />. **Staff stats on every plan.** WinStamp's Statistics show <Fact id="stats.staff" />. Stampeo names who made each scan in its activity feed on every plan, and adds each member's totals on Growth and Pro.

## Where Stampeo is stronger

<Strengths side="stampeo">

**Nothing for customers to install.** A Stampeo customer scans your QR code and adds the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in seconds. No app, no account. WinStamp customers install its app and show its QR code at the counter.

**A notification on every stamp.** The Stampeo card updates itself and shows each stamp, reward and level-up on the lock screen, on Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. WinStamp doesn't publish automatic notifications.

**Points and levels.** Points come with a reward menu that customers redeem in any order, and up to <Fact id="stampeo.levels" /> loyalty levels can give regulars their own card look, perks and a gift, both on every plan. WinStamp's listing describes stamp cards.

**A card designer with a live preview.** Stampeo's card designer handles your logo, colors (or palettes made from your logo), stamp icons, strip background and card back, with a live Apple Wallet and Google Wallet preview. The table above shows what WinStamp's Business app lets you set.

**Your customer list as a file.** The owner can download the customer list as a CSV file on every plan. WinStamp doesn't publish an export.

</Strengths>

## Switching from WinStamp

Customers add the Stampeo card by scanning your QR code; with nothing to install, it takes seconds. Stamps don't carry over on their own, and Stampeo can't import customers. WinStamp's site doesn't describe a data export, so ask its support what you can take with you before you cancel. To honor balances, give every new card a head start with a welcome gift, or add stamps by hand from a customer's profile in the dashboard. Because WinStamp is <Fact id="billing.channel" />, you cancel it from your Apple or Google account, and refunds follow <Fact id="refunds" />. Running both for a few weeks lets regulars move over as they come in. Your Stampeo card can be live the same day.
