Loopy Loyalty puts a stamp card in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet and connects it to your other tools through Zapier, Make and an API. Stampeo puts a card for stamps or points in the same two wallets, with loyalty levels on every plan and the card in four languages, but no integrations.

<ChooseIf side="competitor">

- You want your stamp card linked to other tools through Zapier, Make or an API.
- You run two or three locations and want them on the middle plan, not the top one.
- You want to import existing customers, or add stamps remotely from a web stamper.
- You want to message a single customer, or send location-based messages.

</ChooseIf>

<ChooseIf side="stampeo">

- You want points or loyalty levels as well as stamps, on any plan.
- You want the card in English, Spanish, French or Polish, matching each customer's phone.
- You want milestone messages, and notification wording you can rewrite (Growth and Pro).
- You want to download your customer list on every plan, not only the top one.

</ChooseIf>

## Where Loopy Loyalty is stronger

<Strengths side="competitor">

**Connections to your other tools.** Loopy includes <Fact id="integrations" />, and its homepage describes <Fact id="customers.import" />. It also offers <Fact id="remote.issue" />. Stampeo has no integrations and no public API, and it can't import customers: they join by scanning your QR code, and stamps come from the Scanner app or, by hand, from a customer's profile.

**Messages from the first plan.** Loopy's push messages come with Starter, and its help center describes <Fact id="push.targeting" />. It also offers <Fact id="geofence" />, and it can automate expiry reminders. Stampeo has no broadcasts on Starter, sends them to groups rather than one person, has no location-based messages yet, and doesn't remind customers before a reward expires.

**Several locations below the top plan.** Loopy's Growth plan already covers several locations, and Loopy allows <Fact id="team.users" />. On Stampeo, more than one location needs Pro, and Starter includes <Fact id="stampeo.team.starter" /> team members (you and one more).

</Strengths>

## Where Stampeo is stronger

<Strengths side="stampeo">

**Points and loyalty levels on every plan.** Loopy Loyalty runs stamp cards. Stampeo runs stamps or points: on points, staff type what the customer spent, the Scanner app works out the points, and customers spend them on a reward menu. On either, up to <Fact id="stampeo.levels" /> loyalty levels can give regulars their own card look, perks and a gift.

**A card in your customer's language.** Stampeo's card labels follow the customer's phone in English, Spanish, French or Polish, and you can translate your own wording. Loopy doesn't publish which languages its card and dashboard use; its Stamper app lists <Fact id="languages.staff_app" />.

**Milestones and your own wording.** Every stamp, reward and level-up shows on the lock screen, on Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Growth and Pro add milestone messages ("Halfway there!") and let you rewrite each message.

**Your customer list on every plan.** The owner can download the customer list as a CSV file on any plan. It holds what customers gave at sign-up, not their stamps. Loopy's data export is on Ultimate only.

**No location cap.** Stampeo's Pro covers any number of locations, with no charge per location, which matters once you have more shops than Loopy's Ultimate covers.

</Strengths>

## Switching from Loopy Loyalty

Your customers keep the Stampeo card in the same wallet as Loopy's: they scan your QR code and add it in seconds. Put the printable counter flyer by the till and ask regulars at their next visit. Stamps don't move across on their own, and Stampeo can't import a list. To honor what regulars had collected, give every new card a head start with a welcome gift, or add stamps by hand from a customer's profile in the dashboard.

On Loopy's side, <Fact id="cancel.data" /> when you cancel, so download what you need first (its data export is on Ultimate). Loopy gives <Fact id="refunds" />, so switching close to your renewal date wastes the least. Your Stampeo card can be live the same day.
