Stamp Me runs your stamp card inside its own customer app, with a countertop tap pod, SMS and games on its Pro and Elite plans. Stampeo puts the card straight into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, so customers join from a QR code with nothing to download, and adds loyalty levels on every plan.

- You run several locations and don't want a monthly fee for each one.

## Where Stampeo is stronger

<Strengths side="stampeo">

**Nothing to install.** A Stampeo customer scans your QR code, fills in the short form you choose (or none at all), and adds the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. No app, no account, no password. With Stamp Me today, customers download the app and create an account first; the wallet can then hold their Member Code. Stamp Me's site announces <Fact id="card.wallet_roadmap" />, so check where that stands when you compare.

**A card that notifies by itself.** Every stamp, reward and level-up updates the Stampeo card and shows on the lock screen, on Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, on every plan, Starter included. Stamp Me's push messages start on Pro.

**Loyalty levels on every plan.** Up to <Fact id="stampeo.levels" /> levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold and more), each of which can have its own card look, perks and a one-time gift. Stamp Me doesn't publish levels. If you prefer points, Stampeo's come with a reward menu, basket boosters on Growth and Pro, and a guided switch from stamps that keeps every customer's card.

**Locations with no fee each.** Stampeo's Pro covers any number of locations, with stats, sign-up links and staff for each, and no charge per location.

</Strengths>

## Switching from Stamp Me

Before you leave, download your member list from Stamp Me's Loyalty Portal: it includes each member's active stamps. Customers then join Stampeo by scanning your QR code and adding the card to their wallet. Stampeo can't import the list, and stamps don't carry over on their own. To honor balances, give every new card a head start with a welcome gift, or add stamps by hand from a customer's profile in the dashboard, using your Stamp Me list as the guide. Put the printable flyer at the counter so regulars find it. Stamp Me has no lock-in contract, so you can cancel once your regulars have moved. Your Stampeo card can be live the same day.
