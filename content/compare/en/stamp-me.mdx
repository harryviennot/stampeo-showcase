## Where Stamp Me is stronger

<Strengths side="competitor">

**Two stamp cards at once.** Stamp Me includes <Fact id="program.stamp_cards" />, so a café can run a coffee card and a lunch card side by side. Stampeo runs one program per business.

**Texts and phone support.** Stamp Me's Pro and Elite add SMS, at a small charge per message, and support by phone. Stampeo reaches customers through the card in their wallet, with no SMS, and its support is by email.

<Strengths side="stampeo">

## Switching from Stamp Me

Download your member list from Stamp Me's Loyalty Portal (it includes each member's active stamps) and send it to us: we import it with you, free, and work out how to carry over what each customer had collected. Then put the printable counter flyer by the till; customers scan its QR code and add the Stampeo card to their wallet in seconds. Stamp Me has no lock-in contract, so you can cancel once your regulars have moved. Your card can be live the same day.
