---
title: Roles and what each one can do
description: What an owner, an admin and a scanner can each do in Stampeo, and who can change roles, pause scanning or remove people.
---

Every person on your team has one of three roles. The role decides what they see and what they can change.

## The three roles

**Owner** runs the business, the plan and the team. There is one owner per business. The owner's role can't be changed, paused or removed.

**Admin** sees the dashboard and looks after your customers. The program, the card style and the team stay with the owner. An admin can still add scanners and look after invitations (resend, renew with a new code, cancel). Open a person on the Team page and an admin sees their details but no controls to change them.

**Scanner** scans customer cards from their phone with the Stampeo scanner app. That is all: a scanner has no dashboard, and there is nothing for them to set up.

## Who can change what

| | Owner | Admin | Scanner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add a scanner | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add an admin | Yes | No | No |
| Resend, renew or cancel an invitation | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change someone's role | Yes | No | No |
| Pause or resume someone's scanning | Yes | No | No |
| Remove someone | Yes | No | No |
| See the Team page | Yes | Yes | No |

On the Team page, an admin who opens a person sees the line **Only the owner can change roles and scanning access.**

## Roles and your plan

The **Starter** plan covers two people in total, you and one other, whatever their role. **Growth** and **Pro** have no limit. Pausing someone does not free their seat. See [Add a team member](/docs/team/add-a-team-member).
