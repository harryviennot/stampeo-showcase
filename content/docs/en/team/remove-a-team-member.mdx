title: Remove someone from your team
description: Remove a scanner or an admin from your business, what happens to their access and their past scans, and when to pause instead.

Remove someone who no longer works with you. They lose access to your business straight away, and on a Starter plan their seat becomes free.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can remove people. An admin can open a person but does not see the remove button.
- The owner can't be removed.
- If someone is only away for a while, [pause their scanning](/docs/team/manage-a-team-member) instead. They keep their seat and you can turn it back on.

## Remove someone

1. Open **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Tap the person under **Members**. On a computer, click their row.
3. At the bottom of the panel, under **Danger zone**, tap **Remove from team**.

![The bottom of a team member's panel, with the Scanning access switch and the Remove from team button under Danger zone](/docs/remove-a-team-member-01.png)

4. Read the message and tap **Remove** to confirm.

A message at the bottom of the screen confirms that the person has been removed, and they disappear from your list.

> **Warning:** you can't undo a removal. To bring them back, add them again with a new code or invitation.

## What happens when you remove someone

- **An admin** loses access to your dashboard.
- **A scanner** loses access to the scanner app for your business.
- **Their past scans stay** in Activity, so your history does not change.
- **Their other shops are not affected.** Someone who also works for another business keeps that one, and keeps their Stampeo account.

## Cancel an invitation instead

Someone who has not joined yet is not a member. Find them under **Waiting to join** and tap **Cancel invitation**. See [Add a team member](/docs/team/add-a-team-member).
