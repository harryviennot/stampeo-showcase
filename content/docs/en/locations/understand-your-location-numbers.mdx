title: Understand your location numbers

The **Locations** page shows how each shop is doing and where your customers join. This page explains each number, so you know what you are looking at before you act on it.

> Available on the Pro plan. Owners and admins can see the numbers.

## The list: the last 30 days

Every number on the **Locations** page covers the last 30 days, as the line **Last 30 days** above the cards says. The cards have no period of their own.

![The Locations page: the Last 30 days line, the Main link row and two location cards with their numbers](/docs/understand-your-location-numbers-01.png)

Each location card shows:

- **Scans**: the scans your team made at this location, meaning stamps or points added to a customer's card.
- **Sign-ups**: the customers who joined through this location's QR code.
- **Rewards**: the rewards your team gave out at this location.
- **Last scan**: how long ago the most recent scan or reward at this location was, for example **Last scan 2 hours ago**. If there was none in 30 days, the card says **No scans in the last 30 days**.
Under each name, the card also says how many scanners are assigned to the location, for example **1 scanner**, or **Owners and admins can scan here** when none is assigned.

If the numbers can't load, they show a dash instead of 0. Reload the page to try again.

## The Main link row

The **Main link** row sits at the top of the list, above your locations. It reads **Sign-ups not tied to a location**: the customers who joined in the last 30 days through your main link, not through a location's QR code. Click it to open **Overview**, where the main link and its QR code are.

It answers the question "why are my locations' sign-ups so small?" If **Main link** is large and a shop's **Sign-ups** is small, the customers at that shop are probably scanning the main link's QR code instead of the shop's own. Put the shop's own QR code at its counter: see [Print a QR code for each location](/docs/locations/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location).

Two more things end up under **Main link**:

- If you added your first location recently, the sign-ups from before it, unless you linked your past activity to that location when you added it.
- New sign-ups through the printed QR code of a location you deleted.

The row is not shown when nobody joined through the main link in the last 30 days, or while you are searching the list (the search box appears once you have six locations).

## The panel: choose a period

Click a location to open its panel. The **Activity** section has its own period: **7d**, **30d**, **90d** or **All** (everything on record for this location). It opens on **30d**, so its **Scans** match the card.

![The Activity section of a location, with the period buttons, four numbers and the last scan](/docs/understand-your-location-numbers-02.png)

The four numbers follow the period you picked:

- **Scans**: the same as on the card.
- **Sign-ups here**: the customers who joined through this location's QR code in the period. It is the same number as **Sign-ups** on the card when the period is **30d**.
- **Customers served**: how many different customers had activity at this location in the period, each counted once. A customer who comes ten times counts once here, and each of their scans counts under **Scans**.
- **Rewards redeemed**: the rewards given out at this location in the period.

Under the numbers, a line such as **3 scans cancelled in this period** appears only when you cancelled some. Cancelled scans are shown there, apart from the four numbers. The last-scan line follows the period too: **Last scan 35 minutes ago**, or **No scans in this period**.

Two links lead on from the section:

- **See trends**, at the top right of **Activity**, opens **Analytics** with this location already picked. See [Understanding your analytics](/docs/analytics/understanding-your-analytics).
- **Activity at this location** opens **Activity**, filtered to this location.

Both appear once you have two or more locations.

## Troubleshooting

**A shop's Sign-ups is 0 or very low**

Sign-ups only count customers who joined through that location's own QR code. If your customers use the main link, they appear under **Main link** instead. Check which QR code is at the counter.

**The numbers don't match Analytics**

**Analytics** counts visits, which can merge several scans from the same customer into one, and its periods end yesterday. The **Locations** page counts every scan over the last 30 days, up to now. See [Understanding your analytics](/docs/analytics/understanding-your-analytics).

**I don't see the Main link row**

It is hidden when there were no sign-ups through the main link in the last 30 days, and while a search is active.

**A location I deleted is gone from the list**

A deleted location leaves the list and the filters. Its past scans, sign-ups and rewards stay, under its old name. See [Delete a location](/docs/locations/delete-a-location).
