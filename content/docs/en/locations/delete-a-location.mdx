Delete a location that has closed, or that you no longer want to track on its own. Before you confirm, the message tells you which scanners will be left without a location.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can delete a location. An admin sees the panel without the **Danger zone** section.
- The primary location can't be deleted. To delete it, make another location primary first: see [Edit a location or change the primary one](/docs/locations/edit-a-location).
- Your only location can't be deleted either.
- If a shop is only closed for a while, you don't need to delete anything. Leave the location as it is.

## Delete a location

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. Click the location. Its panel opens.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the panel. Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete location**.
4. Read the message, then click **Delete**. **Keep** closes the message and deletes nothing.

![The delete message for a location, naming a scanner who won't be able to scan anywhere, with Keep and Delete buttons](/docs/delete-a-location-01.png)

A message at the bottom of the screen confirms that the location is deleted, and the panel closes.

> **Warning:** the dashboard has no way to bring a deleted location back. You can add a location again: see "I deleted the wrong location" below.

## What happens when you delete a location

- **Its scanners lose their assignment to it.** A scanner who was assigned only to this location can't scan anywhere until you assign them another. The message names them, for example **Sarah Nguyen won't be able to scan anywhere until you assign another location.** It names three at most, then "and N others". A scanner who is also assigned to another location keeps scanning there, and is not named. After you delete, the notice at the top of **Locations** counts the scanners left without a location: see [Choose who can scan at each location](/docs/locations/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location).
- **Its past activity stays.** Scans, sign-ups and rewards remain on record and keep this location's name. The location leaves your filters, and no longer appears on the **Locations** page.
- **Its printed QR code keeps working.** People who scan it still sign up and get your card. They are counted under **Main link**, not under any location. If a QR code of this location is still on a counter, replace it with another location's QR code so that sign-ups are counted there. See [Print a QR code for each location](/docs/locations/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location).

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Delete location**

Only the owner sees it, and only on a location that is not the primary one. In the panel of the primary location, **Danger zone** says **The primary location can't be deleted. Make another location primary first.** On your only location, it says **Your only location can't be deleted.**

![The Danger zone of the primary location, saying that it can't be deleted](/docs/delete-a-location-02.png)

**I deleted the wrong location**

You can't restore it, but you can add it again. Add the location and give it the same **End of the link** as before: the link of a deleted location is free again, so the QR code you already printed signs people up at the new location. The past activity stays with the deleted location and does not move to the new one, and you need to tick its scanners again. See [Add a location](/docs/locations/add-a-location).

**A scanner can't scan after I deleted a location**

They were assigned only to that location. Tick another location for them: see [Choose who can scan at each location](/docs/locations/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location).

**The message says "Could not delete location"**

Check your connection and try again. Nothing was deleted.
