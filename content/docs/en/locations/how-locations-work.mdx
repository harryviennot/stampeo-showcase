---
title: How locations work and who can change them
description: What a location gives you, how its link differs from your main link, what the primary location means, and who can add, edit and delete locations.
---

If you run more than one shop, locations give each one its own sign-up QR code, its own scanners and its own numbers. This page explains how the pieces fit together. Each task has its own page, linked at the end.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## What a location gives you

Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**. Each location you add has:

- **Its own link and QR code.** Customers who join through it are counted for that location. See [Print a QR code for each location](/docs/locations/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location).
- **Its own scanners.** A scanner can only scan at the locations you tick for them. Owners and admins can scan at every location. See [Choose who can scan at each location](/docs/locations/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location).
- **Its own numbers:** scans, sign-ups and rewards, and when the last scan was. See [Understand your location numbers](/docs/locations/understand-your-location-numbers).
- **An address and a map pin.** The scanner app uses the pin to suggest the nearest location to the person scanning.

## The main link is not a location

Your business also has a main link, with its own QR code, on **Overview**. Customers who join through it, for example from a post on social media, count for no location. They show up in the **Main link** row at the top of the **Locations** page, above your locations.

So that sign-ups are counted for the right shop, put each location's own QR code at its own counter, and keep the main link for places that are not a shop.

## The primary location

The first location you add is the primary one. It is listed first, it is the scanner app's default for staff who haven't picked a location, and it can't be deleted. You can make another location primary whenever you like: see [Edit a location or change the primary one](/docs/locations/edit-a-location).

Primary does not change where sign-ups are counted. Sign-ups through the main link stay under **Main link**.

## Who can change what

Only you, the owner, can change locations. Admins see the page without the buttons and boxes that change anything, and scanners have no dashboard.

| | Owner | Admin | Scanner |
|---|---|---|---|
| See the **Locations** page and its numbers | Yes | Yes | No |
| Download a location's QR code and counter flyer | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add, edit, make primary or delete a location | Yes | No | No |
| Choose who can scan at each location | Yes | No | No |

An admin who opens a location sees who is assigned to it, and the line **The owner decides who scans where.** A scanner sees only the locations they are assigned to, in the scanner app. For the full list of what each role does, see [Roles and what each one can do](/docs/team/roles).

## If you are not on the Pro plan

On the Starter and Growth plans, **Locations** shows an upgrade message instead of your locations, and the sidebar entry carries a **PRO** label.
