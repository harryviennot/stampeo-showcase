A location is one of your shops, with its own QR code, its own scanners and its own numbers. Locations switch on when you have two.

## With one location, nothing changes

Everyone on your team can scan. The **Locations** page shows no numbers and no tick boxes. Everything counts for your one location.

## From two locations, you see each one

Each location shows these numbers for the last 30 days:

- **Scans**: stamps or points your team added there.
- **Sign-ups**: customers who joined through that location's QR code.
- **Rewards**: rewards your team gave out there.

![The Locations page with two locations. Above them, a Main link row. Each location shows scans, sign-ups and rewards.](/docs/how-locations-work-01.png)

Some customers join through your main QR code or link, for example from a social media post. They show in a **Main link** row above your locations. If a shop's **Sign-ups** are low and **Main link** is high, its customers probably scan your main QR code. Put the shop's own QR code at its counter.

## Who scans where

Scanners scan only at the locations you tick for them. They choose their location in the scanner app. A scanner with no location can't scan. Owners and admins can scan at every location. See [Choose your location in the scanner app](/help/choose-your-location-in-the-scanner).

## The primary location

Your first location is the primary one. It is listed first, and the scanner app starts on it for people who haven't picked a location. To make another location the primary one, open it and select **Make primary** under **Details**.

## Delete a location

Open the location and select **Delete location** under **Danger zone**. The message tells you who would be left without a location. Past activity stays under the location's name, and its printed QR code still signs customers up.

You can't delete the primary location. Make another one primary first. If you go back to one location, everyone on your team can scan again.

## Who can change what

| | Owner | Admin | Scanner |
|---|---|---|---|
| See locations, numbers and QR codes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add, edit or delete a location, make one primary | Yes | No | No |
| Choose who scans where | Yes | No | No |

Admins see the same page without the buttons that change anything. The panel says **The owner decides who scans where.** If you are an admin and need a change, ask the owner. For every role, see [Roles and what each one can do](/help/roles).

Locations are part of the Pro plan. On Starter and Growth, **Locations** shows an upgrade message instead.

To add your locations, see [Set up your locations and who scans where](/help/set-up-your-locations).
