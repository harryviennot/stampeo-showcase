---
title: Print a QR code for each location
description: Download each location's QR code as a PNG or PDF, print its counter flyer, and why each shop's own QR code belongs at its own counter.
---

Give each shop its own QR code, so that the customers who scan it are counted for that shop. You can download the QR code as an image or a PDF, or print the counter flyer that carries it.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## Why each location has its own QR code

A customer who adds your card by scanning a location's QR code counts as a sign-up for that location. A customer who joins through your main link, for example from your website or a post on social media, counts for no location. Those sign-ups appear in the **Main link** row at the top of the **Locations** page.

If you put the main link's QR code at a shop's counter, that shop's sign-ups end up under **Main link**, and the shop's own numbers look smaller than they are. Put each location's own QR code at its own counter, and keep the main link for places that are not a shop. See [Understand your location numbers](/docs/locations/understand-your-location-numbers).

## Download a location's QR code

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. Click the location. Its panel opens from the right on a computer and from the bottom on a phone.
3. Under **QR code**, you see the QR code and the link it points to.

![The QR code section of a location, with the link, Copy, Download PNG, Download PDF and the counter flyer row](/docs/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location-01.png)

4. Click **Download PNG** for the QR code as an image, for example to send to a sign maker. Click **Download PDF** for an A4 page with the location's name on top, the QR code in the middle and the link underneath, ready to print.

**Copy** copies the link itself, in case you want to send it to someone who prints your signs.

Owners and admins can both download a location's QR code.

## Print a counter flyer for a location

The counter flyer is a print-ready sheet for the till. It carries the location's name, its QR code, three short steps (scan, add, show) and the Apple Wallet and Google Wallet buttons. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet are the wallet apps already on your customers' phones, where your card lives.

1. In the location's panel, under the QR code, click **Counter flyer for this location**. The **Counter flyer** page opens with that location selected.
2. Under **Language**, pick **Français**, **English**, **Español** or **Polski**. The flyer starts in your business's language.
3. Check **Location**. Change it to print another location's flyer. This field appears once you have two or more locations.
4. Click **Download PDF**. A message at the bottom of the screen says **Flyer downloaded.**

![The Counter flyer page for one location, with the flyer preview and the Language, Location and Download PDF controls](/docs/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location-02.png)

Print it on any A5 or A4 printer and place it where customers pay.

**Download all (one file)** puts the flyers of every location in one PDF, one location per page, in the language you picked.

> **Note:** a flyer you already printed keeps the location's old name. If you rename a location, download its flyer again. The QR code and the link do not change.

## Get every QR code at once

With two or more locations, **Overview** shows a **Your QR codes** card.

1. Open **Overview** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. In **Your QR codes**, click **Download all as PDF** for one A4 page per location, or **Download all as PNG** for a ZIP file with one image per location.
3. To see or download one QR code, click its row. The primary location comes first, and the **Main link** row, marked **For online posts**, comes last.

![The Your QR codes card on Overview, with the two download buttons, one row per location and the Main link row](/docs/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location-03.png)

The two downloads cover your locations only, not the main link. **Manage locations** at the top of the card opens the **Locations** page.

With a single location, **Overview** shows only your main link and its QR code, under **Your program link**. The location's own QR code is in its panel on the **Locations** page.

## Troubleshooting

**Sign-ups at one of my shops show under Main link**

Those customers joined through your main link, not through the shop's QR code. Check that the shop's counter carries its own QR code, and that the link under it ends with that location's name.

**I renamed a location. Does its QR code change?**

No. The name and the address can change, but the link, and so the QR code, stay the same. Anything you printed before keeps working.

**I deleted a location, and its printed QR code is still on a counter**

It still signs people up, and they are counted under **Main link**. See [Delete a location](/docs/locations/delete-a-location).
