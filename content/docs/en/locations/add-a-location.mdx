Add one of your shops as a location, so it gets its own link, QR code and numbers. You do it in a single dialog, and when you finish, the new location opens with its QR code ready to download.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can add a location. An admin sees the **Locations** page without the **Add location** button. See [How locations work and who can change them](/docs/locations/how-locations-work).
- The end of the link is built from the name, and it can't be changed once the location exists. You can still rename the location later.

## Add a location

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. Click **Add location**.

![The top of the Locations page, with the Add location button on the right](/docs/add-a-location-01.png)

3. Type the name under **Location name**, for example "Westside". You and your team see this name in the dashboard and in the scanner app, and it is printed on that location's counter flyer.
4. Look at the link under the name. It starts with your business's own link and ends with the name, in lower case with hyphens. To change the end of it, click **Change** and edit **End of the link**.
5. Under **Search your business**, start typing the name or street address of the shop, then pick it from the list. **Position confirmed** appears with the street, city, postal code and country.
6. Click **Add location**.

![The Add a location dialog with a name typed, the link underneath and the address search](/docs/add-a-location-02.png)

The dialog closes, a message at the bottom of the screen says **Westside added** (with your location's name), and the new location's panel opens. Its **QR code** is at the top: see [Print a QR code for each location](/docs/locations/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location).

### The link

Each location has its own link, which is also what its QR code points to. Customers who join through it are counted for that location.

The link can't be changed after you add the location, because your QR code points to it. A QR code that is already printed or shared would stop working if the link moved. The name and the address can change later without touching the link: see [Edit a location or change the primary one](/docs/locations/edit-a-location).

When you click **Change**, a tick appears next to **End of the link** once the link is free. If another location already uses it, the field says **This link is already used by another location.** and **Add location** stays off until you pick another.

### The address and the map pin

The address is optional. When you pick your shop from the search, Stampeo also saves its position on the map, called the map pin. The scanner app uses the pin to suggest the nearest location to the person scanning.

If your shop does not show up in the search, click **Can't find your business? Enter manually** and fill in **Street**, **City**, **Postal code** and **Country**. **Search with Google instead** takes you back to the search.

> **Note:** an address typed by hand has no map pin. If you correct a field after picking a place (click **Edit details** under **Position confirmed**), the pin is cleared as well, because it no longer matches the address.

## Your first location

If you have no location yet, the dialog starts a little differently, and a few things happen when you add it.

![The Add a location dialog for a first location, with the name and address filled in from the business and the switch to link past activity](/docs/add-a-location-03.png)

- **The name and address start from your business details**, when you gave an address. The dialog says **We've filled this in from your business address. Change it if your shop is somewhere else.**
- **It becomes your primary location.** See [Edit a location or change the primary one](/docs/locations/edit-a-location).
- **Every scanner you already have can scan there.** On a plan with locations, a scanner needs a location to scan, so Stampeo assigns all your existing scanners to your first one. A message confirms it, for example **Xeniká added. Your scanner can scan there.** You can remove anyone in the location's panel: see [Choose who can scan at each location](/docs/locations/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location).
- **Link my past activity to this location** is a switch, off by default. Turn it on if all your activity so far came from this shop. Every past scan, sign-up and reward is then counted for this location, and this can't be undone. If you have several shops, leave it off: your past sign-ups are counted under **Main link**.

After your first location, nobody is assigned to a new location automatically. Tick the people who work there, and do the same for any scanner you add later.

## When you can't click Add location

The button stays pale until the form is complete. A line under the buttons says what is missing:

- **Give the location a name to continue.**
- **Choose the end of the link to continue.**
- **Checking that this link is free…** (wait a moment)
- **This link is already used. Choose another to continue.**

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Add location**

Only the owner sees it. If **Locations** shows an upgrade message instead of your locations, your plan does not include them yet: locations are part of the Pro plan.

**The link is already used by another location**

Two locations of the same business can't share a link, so the end of the link opens by itself with an error. Pick another end, for example the street or the district. The link of a location you deleted is free again.

**I want a different link and the location is already added**

The link can't change. If you have not printed or shared its QR code yet, you can delete the location and add it again with the link you want: see [Delete a location](/docs/locations/delete-a-location). Otherwise keep the link and change only the name.
