---
title: Choose who can scan at each location
description: Tick the scanners who work at a location, what happens to a scanner with no location, and why owners and admins can scan everywhere.
---

A scanner can only scan at the locations you tick for them. You choose from a location's own panel, with one tick per person, and the change saves at once.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can tick. An admin sees who is assigned to a location, and the line **The owner decides who scans where.**
- The person has to be on your team first. See [Add a team member](/docs/team/add-a-team-member).
- Owners and admins are not in the list: they can scan at every location, and the panel says so under the list: **Owners and admins can scan at every location.**

## Tick the people who work at a location

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. Click the location.
3. Find **Who can scan here**. Every scanner on your team is listed with a box.
4. Tick the box next to each person who works at this location. Untick it to take them off.

![The Who can scan here list, with one scanner ticked and one not](/docs/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location-01.png)

A message at the bottom of the screen confirms each change, for example **Sarah Nguyen can now scan here** or **Sarah Nguyen can no longer scan here**. The row stays where it is when you tick it, so if you tapped the wrong person, tap again to undo it.

The people already assigned to the location come first, then the others, in alphabetical order. With more than eight scanners, the list shows the assigned ones and a **Show N more** row that opens the rest.

You can do the same from the other side: open a person on the Team page and tick their locations. Both screens show the same thing. See [Manage a team member](/docs/team/manage-a-team-member).

A scanner who is assigned to several locations picks the one they are working at in the scanner app, and the app remembers it. For a location with a map pin, the app can also suggest the nearest one.

## When a scanner has no location

On the Pro plan, a scanner with no location can't scan at all. You find out in three places:

- **At the top of the Locations page**, a notice says **1 scanner isn't assigned to a location and can't scan yet.** Click **Assign a location** to open that person on the Team page. With several scanners, the notice counts them and the link is **Open the team**. Paused scanners are not counted.
- **In the list under a location**, the person's row says **Can't scan: no location** in orange.
- **In the scanner app**, on a business with several locations, they see **No location assigned**, and who to ask.

![The warning at the top of the Locations page, saying that one scanner isn't assigned to a location and can't scan yet](/docs/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location-02.png)

![The Who can scan here list, with a scanner marked Can't scan: no location](/docs/choose-who-can-scan-at-each-location-03.png)

Tick any location for that person and they can scan there straight away.

Two cases leave a scanner without a location:

- **A scanner you add after your first location** has none until you tick one.
- **A scanner whose only location you deleted.** See [Delete a location](/docs/locations/delete-a-location).

Your existing scanners are assigned to your first location automatically, so they keep scanning when you add it. See [Add a location](/docs/locations/add-a-location).

## If you have no scanners yet

The section says **No scanners on your team yet.** with an **Add a scanner** link that opens the Team page.

## Troubleshooting

**A scanner says they can't scan**

Open **Locations** and look for **Can't scan: no location** under their name. Tick a location for them. If the app still shows **No location assigned**, ask them to close and reopen it.

**I ticked a location and they still can't scan**

Look for **Paused** under their name. A paused scanner can't scan anywhere. See [Manage a team member](/docs/team/manage-a-team-member) to turn scanning back on.

**I don't see the boxes**

Only the owner sees them. An admin sees the people assigned to each location, each linking to their page on the Team page.

**Someone is missing from the list**

The list shows scanners only. Add the person on the Team page first, with the **Scanner** role. See [Add a team member](/docs/team/add-a-team-member).
