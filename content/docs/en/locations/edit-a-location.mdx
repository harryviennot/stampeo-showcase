title: Edit a location or change the primary one
description: Change a location's name or address, what the map pin does, what the primary location means, and how to make another location primary.
---

Correct a location's name or address, or choose which location is the primary one. The link and the QR code never change, so anything you already printed keeps working.

> Available on the Pro plan.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can edit a location or make another one primary. An admin sees the same panel without the **Edit details** and **Make primary** buttons. See [How locations work and who can change them](/docs/locations/how-locations-work).

## Edit the name or the address

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar, under **Loyalty Program**.
2. Click the location. Its panel opens.
3. Scroll to **Details**, which shows the **Address** and the **Map pin**, and click **Edit details**.

![The Details section of a location, with the address, the map pin and the Edit details and Make primary buttons](/docs/edit-a-location-01.png)

4. Change the **Location name**, the address, or both.
5. Click **Save**. A message at the bottom of the screen says **Location updated**.

![The Edit location dialog, with the name, the link that can't change, and the address](/docs/edit-a-location-03.png)

The link under the name stays as it is: **The link can't be changed, because your QR code points to it.** Renaming a location does not affect what you printed, except the counter flyer, which carries the name: download it again. See [Print a QR code for each location](/docs/locations/print-a-qr-code-for-each-location).

### The map pin

**Map pin** says **Set** or **Not set**. The scanner app suggests the nearest location from it. The pin is set when you pick your shop from **Search your business**. If you type the address by hand, or correct a field by hand, the pin is cleared, because it no longer matches the address, and **Map pin** then says **Not set**.

To set it again, open **Edit details** and pick your shop from the search. If the dialog opens on the **Street**, **City**, **Postal code** and **Country** fields, click **Search with Google instead** first.

## What primary means

One of your locations is the primary one. It carries a **Primary** label, with an "i" next to it in its panel.

![The top of the primary location's panel, with the Primary label and its explanation](/docs/edit-a-location-04.png)

The primary location:

- **is listed first**, on the **Locations** page and in your QR codes;
- **is the scanner app's default** for staff who haven't picked a location;
- **can't be deleted**.

Your first location is the primary one. There is always exactly one. Primary does not decide where sign-ups are counted: customers who join through your main link stay under **Main link**, never under the primary location.

## Make another location primary

1. Open the location that should become the primary one. A location that is already primary has no **Make primary** button.
2. Under **Details**, click **Make primary**.
3. Read the message, which asks whether to make this location the primary one, then click **Make primary** again to confirm. **Cancel** closes it without changing anything.

![The confirmation asking whether to make the location the primary one](/docs/edit-a-location-02.png)

A message says that the location is now the primary one, and the list puts it first. The location that was primary stops being primary, and you can delete it from now on.

Phones that already chose a location in the scanner app keep their choice. The change applies to staff who haven't picked one.

## Troubleshooting

**I don't see Edit details or Make primary**

Only the owner sees them. **Make primary** is also missing on the location that is already primary.

**Map pin says Not set after I changed the address**

You typed or corrected the address by hand. Open **Edit details** and pick your shop from **Search your business** to set the pin again.

**I want to change the link**

The link can't change, because your QR code points to it. Keep the link and change only the name. If nothing has been printed or shared yet, you can delete the location and add it again with the link you want: see [Delete a location](/docs/locations/delete-a-location).

**The message says "Could not save the location." or "Couldn't change the primary location"**

Check your connection and try again. Nothing was changed.
