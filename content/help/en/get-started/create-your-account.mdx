Your account takes about a minute to create. Then you set up your business and your card.

## Sign up

1. Go to stampeo.app and select **Get started**.
2. Type **Your name**. Your phone number is optional: we only use it for urgent account issues.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with email**.
5. With email, type your address and a password, then select **Continue**. The password needs at least 6 characters, with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol.
6. Type the six-digit code we email you, then select **Verify**.

With Google or Apple, you skip steps 5 and 6. After one last question, how you found us, you land in your dashboard, ready to set up your card.

<Details summary="You opened the page from Instagram or Facebook">
Google and Apple sign-in don't work inside the Instagram or Facebook app. The page shows you how to open it in your phone's browser: tap **•••** at the top right, then **Open in external browser**. Or select **Use email instead**.
</Details>

<Details summary="Someone invited you to their team">
You don't need to create a business. If they gave you a code, use it in the scanner app. If they sent you an email link, open it to join their dashboard. See [Add a team member](/help/add-a-team-member#for-the-person-being-invited).
</Details>

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="The code didn't arrive">
It can take a minute, and it sometimes lands in spam. On the code screen, select **Resend code**. If you made a typo in your address, select **Use a different email**.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I already have an account with this email">
Sign in instead: on the first screen, select **Sign in** next to **Already have an account?**. Use the same method as last time, Google, Apple or email. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
</Symptom>
