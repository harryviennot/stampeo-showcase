Your loyalty card lives on your customers' phones, in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. They add it in a few seconds, with no app to download. It updates by itself every time your team scans it.

## Three pieces

- **The card** sits in the wallet app that's already on your customers' phones: Apple Wallet on iPhone, Google Wallet on Android. It shows their stamps or points and your reward.
- **The scanner app** is the free Stampeo app your team uses at the counter. It scans the card and adds stamps or points.
- **The dashboard** at app.stampeo.app is where you set up the card, see your customers and manage your team.

## Two QR codes, two jobs

This is what confuses most people at first.

| QR code | Where it is | Who scans it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your shop's QR code | On your counter, your flyer, your social media | A customer, with their phone camera | Opens your sign-up page, so they can get the card |
| The QR code on the card | On each customer's card, in their wallet | Your team, with the scanner app | Finds that customer and adds their stamps or points |

Customers never stamp their own card. The scanner app only reads the QR code on a customer's card. If it says "Invalid QR code", someone has usually scanned your shop's QR code instead.

## A visit, step by step

1. A new customer scans your shop's QR code and fills in a short form. By default, it asks for their name and email.
2. The card opens on their phone, and they add it to their wallet in one tap.
3. On their next visit, they show the card. Someone on your team scans it with the scanner app and adds a stamp. For points, they type what the customer spent.
4. The card updates on the customer's phone, usually within a few seconds, with a short message on their lock screen.
5. When the card is full, or they have enough points, your team gives them their reward from the scanner app.

## Stamps or points

- **Stamps:** one stamp per visit, or several at once, and a reward when the card is full. Just like a paper card.
- **Points:** customers earn points for what they spend, then swap them for a reward from your menu.
- **Levels:** optional on either. Your best regulars move up to a better card as they earn.

You choose one when you set up your card. See [Stamps or points: how each one works](/help/stamps-or-points).

## Who does what

- **You, the owner,** set up the card and manage your plan.
- **Admins** help you look after your customers in the dashboard.
- **Scanners** use the scanner app at the counter, and nothing else.

See [Roles and what each one can do](/help/roles).
