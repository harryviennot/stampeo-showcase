Your card runs on stamps or on points, never both at once. Both work on every plan.

## Stamps

Customers collect one stamp per visit, just like a paper card. When the card is full, they get your reward.

- You choose how many stamps fill the card, from 2 to 21, and what the reward is.
- Your team can add several stamps in one scan, for a group or a bigger order.
- With **Stackable rewards** switched on, a full card saves the reward and starts again, so a customer never loses a stamp.

Stamps suit shops where most visits look alike: a coffee, a haircut, a car wash.

## Points

Customers earn points for what they spend. Your team types the amount on the receipt, and the app works out the points.

- You choose how many points a customer earns for each euro or dollar they spend.
- You build a menu of up to 12 rewards, each with its price in points. Customers can spend points on a small reward or save up for a bigger one.
- On Growth and Pro, you can also give bonus points on bigger purchases.

Points suit shops where baskets vary a lot: a bakery, a bookshop, a clothes shop.

For help choosing, see [Stamps or points: which loyalty program fits your shop](/loyalty-programs).

## What your customers see

- **A stamps card** shows how many stamps they have, for example 4 of 10, and the reward they're working towards.
- **A points card** shows their balance and how close they are to their next reward.

## Changing your mind later

You can switch from one to the other later, in your program settings. The switch converts every customer's balance and can't be undone, so it's worth choosing carefully now.
