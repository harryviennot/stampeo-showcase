Switching off puts everyone back on your first card. Nobody loses stamps, points or rewards. Your levels and their cards are kept, so you can switch them back on.

## Switch levels off

1. Open **Overview** in the sidebar.
2. On the **Levels** card, select **Switch off**.
3. Turn on **Announce it on their card** to send customers a short message that your levels have ended.
4. Select **Switch off levels**.

Cards update on your customers' phones in the background. You can leave the page. When it ends, you see **Levels are off**.

## What changes

- Every customer is on your first card. The stamps on their card come with them.
- Rewards they hold stay valid.
- Levels disappear from the **Customers** page, the scanner app, your sign-up page and **Analytics**.
- You can't send a broadcast to a level, and the level-up message stops.

<Callout type="note">
If you switch between stamps and points while levels are off, your kept levels are deleted. They would be the wrong type.
</Callout>

## Switch levels back on

1. On **Overview**, the card now reads **Your levels are switched off**. Select **Set up levels again**.
2. The guide opens with your kept levels. Go through it as in [Set up levels](/help/set-up-levels).

Choose **By their history** to place customers again. Each customer gets the level their visits earn. Levels you gave by hand are not restored.

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="It says &quot;You announced levels recently&quot;">
You can announce levels once every 7 days, including when you switch them on or off. Select **Switch off without announcing**, or wait until the date shown.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;The update stopped&quot;">
We couldn't finish switching levels off. Open **Overview** to see where things stand, then switch off again.
</Symptom>
