Levels give your best regulars a better card. Customers start on your first level and move up as they earn. Every plan includes levels, up to 5.

## What a level is

Each level has:

- **A card.** Its own colors and, on a stamps card, its own size and reward.
- **A rule to move up.** What a customer must earn to reach the next level.
- **A gift,** if you want one. A one-time reward when a customer reaches the level.
- **Perks,** if you want some. Up to 3 short lines, like "Free refill", that your team applies at the till.

You name the levels. The defaults are Bronze, Silver and Gold. Your first level is the card you already have. Customers who haven't moved up see the card they know.

## How customers move up

A customer moves up when they earn. Spending or redeeming never moves them.

- **Stamps cards:** you choose a number of full cards. With 2 full cards of 10 stamps, a customer moves up after earning 20 stamps on that level.
- **Points cards:** you choose a number of points. A customer moves up after earning that many points on the level. Spending points on a reward doesn't slow them down.

The top level has no rule. Customers who reach it keep it.

## What carries over

- **Stamps and points.** The stamps on a customer's card come with them onto the new card. If they fill the new card, the reward is saved. A points balance never changes when someone moves up.
- **Earned rewards.** A reward earned on a lower level stays valid.
- **Levels only go up.** If you make a rule harder, nobody moves back. The one exception is a mistake. If you undo the scan that moved a customer up, they return to their old level. The level gift is taken back.
- **Your program type.** If you switch between stamps and points, your levels convert too. Customers keep their level.

## What changes when levels are on

- A full stamps card always saves its reward and starts a new card. **Stackable rewards** turns on and stays on.
- Your program settings edit your first level only. The other levels have their own cards.
- Customers see their level name on their card. When they move up, the card changes look and a message arrives on their lock screen.
- Your sign-up page shows your levels under the card.
- Your team sees the level and any perks in the scanner app before they add a stamp.
