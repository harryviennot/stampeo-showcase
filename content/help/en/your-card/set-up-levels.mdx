Levels give your regulars a better card as they earn. You set them up once, in a full-screen guide. If you leave, your draft is kept: select **Continue setup** to pick it up.

## Before you start

- You can have 2 to 5 levels, on every plan.
- Only you, the owner, can set up levels. Admins can see them once they are on.
- New to levels? Read [How levels work](/help/how-levels-work).

## Set up your levels

1. Open **Overview** in the sidebar, then select **Set up levels**.
2. On **Choose your levels**, pick the **Number of levels** and name each one. Set what it takes to move up.
3. On **What each level gives**, set the reward of each level above the first. Add a gift or perks if you like.
4. On **Level cards**, check each card. Select one to change its colors, then select **Done**.
5. On **Where do your customers start?**, choose how to place your current customers.
6. On **The level-up message**, read the message customers get when they move up.
7. On **Review your levels**, select **Turn on levels**, then confirm.

Select **Continue** at the end of each screen. If you have no customers yet, step 5 is skipped.

<Callout type="note">
On Starter, customers get the default level-up message and you can't change it. Growth and Pro can edit it, and can set how many days a gift stays valid.
</Callout>

<Details summary="What to enter for each level">
- **Move-up rule:** **Full cards to move up** on a stamps card, **Points to earn to move up** on a points card. Under each level, a line shows about how many visits and months a new customer needs. It follows how often your regulars visit.
- **First level:** it is your current card. Its reward and size are edited in your program settings. Only its move-up rule is set here.
- **Name:** up to 16 characters.
- **Stamps on the card:** 1 to 21, for each level above the first.
- **Gift:** turn on **Give a gift on reaching this level** and name the gift. Customers get it once, when they move up.
- **Perks:** your team applies them at the till. Up to 3, each up to 40 characters.
- **Level cards:** each level keeps your logo and texts. Only the colors and the stamp count change. If your card uses a photo, every level keeps the same look.
</Details>

<Details summary="Add the level or the gift to the message">
On Growth and Pro, add these with the chips in the editor:

- **Current level**
- **Next level**
- **Stamps left to the next level** or **Points left to the next level**
- **Perks of the current level**
- **The gift just given, as a sentence**, which only works in this message
</Details>

## Your current customers

If you have customers, choose where they start:

- **By their history.** Each customer starts on the level their past visits earn. Their cards update on their phones.
- **Everyone starts on** your first level. Nobody moves yet. Customers move up on their next visits.

<Details summary="The two switches on this screen">
With **By their history**, two switches can appear.

- **Give level gifts now** is off by default. When on, customers placed on a higher level get the gifts of the levels they skip. A customer placed on Gold gets the Silver and Gold gifts.
- **Tell customers who move up** is on by default. They get your level-up message once.

A full card that was waiting to be claimed is saved as a reward when you turn levels on. Customers keep every stamp.
</Details>

## What happens next

Customers are placed and their cards update on their phones in the background. You can leave the page. When it ends, you see **Levels are on**.

From now on, a full stamps card saves its reward and starts a new one. To explain levels to your customers, send them [Move up levels on your loyalty card](/help/move-up-levels).

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="Continue stays grey on Level cards">
Your level cards are still being made. Wait until **Making your level cards. This takes a moment.** goes away. If you see **We couldn't make the level cards.**, select **Try again**. On the review screen, **Go to level cards** takes you back.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;The update stopped&quot;">
Levels are on, but we couldn't finish placing everyone. Customers who weren't placed yet move up on their next visit. Open the **Levels** card on **Overview** to check where things stand.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;You announced levels recently&quot;">
You can tell all customers about levels once every 7 days, including when you switch levels on or off. Select **Turn on without announcing**, or wait until the date shown.
</Symptom>
