Send a broadcast to only the customers on the levels you pick, like your Gold customers.

## Before you start

- Levels must be on. See [Set up levels](/help/set-up-levels).
- Only you, the owner, can send broadcasts.

## Pick a level

1. Open **Broadcasts** in the sidebar, then select **New broadcast**.
2. Write your message on **Compose**, then select **Next**.
3. On **Audience**, select **Filtered audience**.
4. Under **Level**, select one or more levels. Each shows its customers, for example **Silver · 12**.
5. Check the estimate, then select **Next** and finish as usual.

The estimate counts every customer on the levels you picked. You can pick several levels.

<Callout type="note">
The level filter is included on Growth and Pro. Filters by stamps, points, rewards, last visit or location are on Pro.
</Callout>

<OpenInDashboard path="/program/broadcasts" label="Open Broadcasts" />

<Details summary="You remove a level later">
A scheduled broadcast aimed at a removed level loses that level. If it has no level left, it goes back to draft. Pick a level again before you schedule it.
</Details>

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="There is no Level group">
The **Level** group only appears under **Filtered audience**, and only while levels are on.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;Levels are off, so you can't send to a level&quot;">
Levels are off. Take the level out of the audience, or [switch levels back on](/help/switch-off-levels).
</Symptom>
