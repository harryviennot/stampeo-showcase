Every plan includes the loyalty card, the scanner app and the dashboard, for as many customers as you like. The plans differ in what you can do with them. Prices depend on your country, so they're on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## On every plan

- A stamps or points card, in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet
- The scanner app for your team
- No limit on customers
- Automatic messages on your customers' lock screen when they get a stamp or a reward, which you can switch on or off
- Rewards that keep the card going, gifts, and a welcome bonus for new customers
- Up to 5 levels, so your best regulars move up to a better card
- Your customer list, and exporting it
- Your QR code and a counter flyer to print
- Analytics for the last 30 days

## Where the plans differ

| | Starter | Growth | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| People on your team, you included | 2 | No limit | No limit |
| Your own card styles | 1 | No limit | No limit |
| Broadcasts | – | 8 a month | No limit |
| Schedule broadcasts for later | – | – | Yes |
| Choose who gets a broadcast | – | Everyone, by when they joined, or by level | Also by stamps, points, rewards, last visit or location |
| Write your own automatic messages | – | Yes | Yes |
| Milestone messages | – | 3 | No limit |
| Rewards that expire | – | Yes | Yes |
| A limit on what one customer earns a day or week | – | Yes | Yes |
| Bonus points on bigger purchases | – | 1 level | Up to 6 levels |
| Your own questions on the sign-up form | – | 5 | 10 |
| See what each team member scanned | – | Yes | Yes |
| Analytics history | 30 days | 90 days | 1 year |
| Analytics charts | – | Yes | Yes |
| Monthly cohorts in analytics | – | – | Yes |
| Several locations, each with its own QR code and numbers | – | – | Yes |

A few details:
- **Team:** someone you've paused still counts. An invitation that hasn't been accepted yet doesn't.
- **Card styles:** seasonal card styles from Stampeo don't count toward your limit.
- **Broadcasts:** the 8 on Growth start again on the 1st of each month. Drafts don't count.
- **Rewards that expire** work on stamp cards only.
- **Levels:** every plan can have up to 5. On Growth and Pro, you can also edit the level-up message and give a level gift an expiry date.
- **Bonus points** work on points cards only.

## If you change plan

Moving up applies straight away. Moving down applies at the end of your billing period. Anything the new plan doesn't cover is paused, never deleted.
