Your shop's loyalty card has levels. The more you earn, the higher your level, and the better your card gets.

## How it works

1. Collect stamps or points as usual. The team adds them when you pay.
2. When you've earned enough on your level, you move up. Your card gets a new look and shows your new level.
3. You may get a message on your phone. Some levels also come with a one-time gift.
4. Keep going to reach the top level. You keep it.

## Good to know

- You keep your stamps, points and rewards when you move up. The stamps already on your card come with you.
- A reward you earned on a lower level still works.
- Your level never goes down because you visit less often.
- Some levels have perks, like a free refill. The team sees them when they scan your card and applies them when you pay.

## See your levels

Open your card in your wallet app and look at its details. Find **How the levels work**. The shop's sign-up page shows them too, under **Your card levels up**.
