Your business name and logo appear on your sign-up page, in the emails Stampeo sends for you, and in your team's scanner app. Your brand color is the accent color of your dashboard, your sign-up page and your team invitations. You change all three in **Settings**, under **Business profile**.

<Callout type="note">
These don't change your card. The title, logo and colors on your customers' cards are set in **Card styles**, and there is a **Card style** link under this section to get there.
</Callout>

> Only the owner can open **Settings**. Admins and people who scan don't see it.

![The Business profile section, with the logo and its Change, Adjust crop and Remove buttons, the business name and the brand color](/help/change-your-business-name-logo-and-color-01.png)

## Change your logo

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar and find **Business profile**.
2. Under **Logo**, click **Change** and choose an image. It must be a PNG or JPG file of 2 MB or less. Other formats, like WebP or HEIC, are refused: save the image as PNG or JPG first.
3. In **Crop your logo**, drag the corners to keep the part you want, from square up to three times as wide as it is tall. Click **Apply**.

The new logo saves straight away, with no need to click **Save changes**. To crop your current logo again, click **Adjust crop**.

### Remove your logo

1. Under **Logo**, click **Remove**.
2. Settings asks **Remove your logo?** Click **Remove logo** to confirm, or **Keep logo** to leave it.

![The Remove your logo? confirmation, with Keep logo and Remove logo](/help/change-your-business-name-logo-and-color-02.png)

Without a logo, your sign-up page shows the first letters of your business name on your brand color. You can upload a logo again whenever you like.

## Change your business name

1. In **Business profile**, type the new name in **Business name**.
2. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen. A message says **Changes saved.**

A business always needs a name. If you clear the field and click **Save changes**, the field says **A name is required.** and nothing is saved.

## Change your brand color

1. In **Business profile**, find **Brand color**. The swatches before the small divider come from your card, so your dashboard can match it. The others are colors that stay easy to read on the dashboard.
2. Click a swatch. To use another color, type its code in the field next to **#**, or click **+** to pick one. Your dashboard shows the new color right away, so you can judge it before saving.
3. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen.

If you leave the page or click **Discard** without saving, your dashboard goes back to the color it had.

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="I changed my logo but my customers' cards still show the old one">

The logo on your cards is part of your card style, not of your business profile. Open **Card styles** in the sidebar, or the **Card style** link under **Business profile**, and change it there.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="My logo was refused">

Only PNG and JPG files of 2 MB or less are accepted. Save the image as PNG or JPG, or make it smaller, and try again.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="My brand color went back to the old one">

The color was only previewed. Pick it again and click **Save changes** before leaving the page.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I don't see Settings">

Only the owner of the business can open **Settings**. Ask the owner to make the change.

</Symptom>
