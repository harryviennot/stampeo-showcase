The back of your customers' cards shows your opening hours and how to reach you. Change them once in **Settings** and every card already in your customers' phones updates with the new details.

> Only the owner can open **Settings**. Admins and people who scan don't see it.

## Change your hours or a contact detail

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The first card, **On your customers' cards**, lists every line on the back of your card, with what it says today under its name.

![The On your customers' cards section, with the Opening Hours, Website and Phone lines and the Add info button](/help/update-your-opening-hours-and-contact-details-01.png)

2. Click the line you want to change, for example **Opening Hours**. It opens right there.
3. Make your change.
   - For a website, phone, email or address, type the new text.
   - For **Opening Hours**, each row is a set of days with its opening and closing times. Type the days the way you want them to read, for example "Mon-Fri". Then set the two times, or turn on **Closed** for a day you don't open. **Add time slot** adds a row, and the cross at the end of a row removes it. A lunch break is two rows for the same days.

![The Opening Hours line open, with Saturday's closing time changed to 17:00 and the Closed switch on for Sunday](/help/update-your-opening-hours-and-contact-details-02.png)

4. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen.

![The bar at the bottom of the page, with Discard and Save changes](/help/update-your-opening-hours-and-contact-details-04.png)

A message says **Changes saved.** Cards already in your customers' wallets update on their own, usually within a few minutes. New customers get the new details straight away.

Changed your mind before saving? Click **Discard** to put everything back the way it was. If you open another page of the dashboard with changes you haven't saved, Settings asks before leaving.

## Add, remove or reorder a line

- **Add a line:** click **Add info** and choose what to add: **Opening Hours**, **Website**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Address** or **Custom Field**. Each kind appears once on the card, except **Custom Field**, which you can add as often as you like, for example for your parking or your terms. Fill it in, then click **Save changes**.
- **Remove a line:** open it, click **Remove**, then **Save changes**.
- **Change the order:** open a line and use the up and down arrows. The card shows the lines in the same order.

A line with nothing in it says **Won't show on your card**. It stays off the back of the card until you fill it in.

## See what your customers see

The preview shows the back of your current card with your changes in it, even before you save.

- **On a wide screen:** it sits beside the list, under **Back of your card**.
- **On a phone or a narrow window:** click **Preview** at the top of the **On your customers' cards** section.

![The Back of your card preview, showing the opening hours, website and phone as customers see them](/help/update-your-opening-hours-and-contact-details-03.png)

## Lines for one card only

The lines in **Settings** go on the back of every card you offer. To add a line to one card only, or to hide one of these lines on a particular card, open **Card style** from the link under the list. A line hidden on your current card says **Hidden on your card** in **Settings**, and it doesn't appear in the preview.

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="My customers still see the old hours">

Check that you clicked **Save changes**: leaving the page without saving keeps the old details. Once saved, cards update on their own, usually within a few minutes. If one line never appears, it's probably hidden on that card: look for **Hidden on your card** and change it in **Card style**.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I clicked Save changes and the page jumped to another section">

**Save changes** saves everything on the page at once. If something elsewhere needs fixing first, like an empty business name or an address you started and didn't finish, nothing is saved and the page scrolls to it. Fix it, then click **Save changes** again.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I don't see Settings">

Only the owner of the business can open **Settings**. Ask the owner to make the change.

</Symptom>
