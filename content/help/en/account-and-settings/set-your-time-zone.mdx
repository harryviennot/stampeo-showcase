Your business time zone decides where one day ends and the next begins. Your analytics use it to place every visit on the right day and hour, and so does the daily limit you can set for each customer. Stampeo picks one for you, and you can correct it in a few clicks.

> Only the owner can change the time zone. The **Settings** page is not available to admins or to people who scan.

## How Stampeo picks it

When you create your business, we start from the time zone of the device you are using. Until you set it yourself, Stampeo can also refine it from your country, from your shop's address, and from the time zone of your browser when you open the dashboard. A guess that comes from your browser is never taken from a device set to plain UTC, because that says nothing about where you are.

A time zone you choose in **Settings** always wins. After that, Stampeo never changes it on its own.

## Check or change it

1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the card called **Address and time zone**, and find **Time zone** in it.

![The Address and time zone section, with the Country and Time zone fields side by side](/help/set-your-time-zone-01.png)

3. Click the field. The list opens with your business's current time zone first, then your browser's if it is different, then all the others.
4. Type the name of a big city in your region in **Search time zones…**, for example "Paris" or "Chicago", and click the one you want.

![The time zone list with "Paris" typed in the search and Paris (GMT+1) found](/help/set-your-time-zone-02.png)

5. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen. A message says **Changes saved.**

Until you save, nothing changes: if you leave the page or click **Discard**, your business keeps its time zone.

## What changes

- **Your analytics are counted again.** Past visits move to the day and hour they really happened in your new time zone: a visit at 11 pm can land on the next day if the zone moves forward. On **Analytics**, a message says **Recalculating your analytics in your new time zone** and the page updates by itself, usually within about ten minutes.
- **Your daily limits reset at midnight in the new zone.** The **Most per day** limit, under **Earning limit per customer** in your program settings, restarts at midnight in your business time zone.

Nothing else moves. Your cards, your customers, their stamps and their points stay as they are.

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="My busiest hour looks a few hours off">

Your time zone is probably not the one you expect. Open **Settings**, check **Time zone**, change it if needed and click **Save changes**. The hours on **Analytics** follow it.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I cannot find my country in the list">

The list is made of cities, not countries, and the search matches city and region names. Search for a large city in your time zone instead. If you are not sure which, use the one your browser suggests: it is pinned at the top of the list.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I do not see Settings, or the Time zone field">

Only the owner can open **Settings**. Ask the owner of the business to change it.

</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I changed it and Analytics still says it is recalculating">

The recalculation runs about every ten minutes and the page updates when it finishes. Nothing is lost while you wait.

</Symptom>
