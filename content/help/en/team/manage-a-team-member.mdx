Everything you can do about one person lives in a single panel that opens when you tap them on the Team page: their role, their scans, their locations, and whether they can scan.

## Before you start

- Only you, the owner, can change a role or pause scanning. An admin can open the same panel but only to read it, and sees the line **Only the owner can change roles and scanning access.**
- To add someone first, see [Add a team member](/help/add-a-team-member).

![An admin's view of a team member: their role as text, the owner-only line, Activity, and no controls](/help/manage-a-team-member-02.png)

## Open someone

1. Open **Team** in the sidebar.
2. Tap a person under **Members**. On a computer, click their row.

The panel opens from the right on a computer and from the bottom on a phone. Each person's status sits under their name in the list:

- **Last scan** followed by how long ago, such as **Last scan 2 hours ago**.
- **No scans yet** if they have not scanned.
- **Paused** if you have turned their scanning off.
- **Can't scan: no location** on a business with several locations, when a scanner is not assigned to any (see below).

![A team member's panel, with Role, Activity, Scanning access and Remove from team](/help/manage-a-team-member-01.png)

## Change a role

1. Open the person.
2. Under **Role**, tap **Scanner** or **Admin**.

The role changes straight away and a message at the bottom of the screen confirms it. You do not need to add the person again.

## Pause or resume scanning

Pausing stops someone scanning without removing them, for example while they are away.

1. Open the person.
2. Under **Scanning access**, turn **Allowed to scan** off. Turn it on again to resume.

A paused person who tries to scan sees that their scanning access has been paused for this business. They keep their seat and their place in the list, and the panel shows a note that scanning is paused.

<Callout type="note">
On the **Starter** plan, turning scanning back on when your team is already at its limit pauses another person to make room. Stampeo asks you to confirm first with the message **Activating this member will pause another.**
</Callout>

## See their activity

The **Activity** section shows when they **Joined**, their **Last scan** and their **All-time scans**.

> Last scan and all-time scans are available on Growth and Pro plans. On Starter, the section shows an upgrade message instead, and the list shows their email under their name.

All-time scans counts the scans they made with the scanner app for your business, including scans made offline and synced later. Scans you cancel and stamps you add yourself from the dashboard are not counted. The figures are shown to you and to your admins only, never to other scanners, and Stampeo does not rank people against each other.

## Choose where they work

If you run several locations on the **Pro** plan, a scanner can only scan at the locations they are assigned to. The **Locations** section of their panel lists every location you have, with a box next to each one.

1. Tap the person on the Team page.
2. In **Locations**, tick each location they work at. The change is saved at once.
3. To take them off a location, untick it.

A scanner with no location ticked can't scan anywhere: their row and their panel show **Can't scan: no location** until you tick one. You can also assign people from the other side, in a location's own panel on the **Locations** page; both stay in sync.

Owners and admins can scan at every location, so they have nothing to tick. Admins see where each scanner works but only the owner can change it.
