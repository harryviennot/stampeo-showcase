Each customer's level shows on the **Customers** page. Owners and admins can also move a customer to another level, up or down.

## See who is on each level

- On **Overview**, the **Levels** card lists each level with its customers. Select a level to open them.
- On **Customers**, each customer has a chip with their level. Use the **Level** filter to show one level. **All levels** shows everyone.
- On **Analytics**, **Levels** shows the split. **Moves up per month** shows how many customers moved up.

## Change a customer's level

1. Open **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Select the customer.
3. Under their card, select **Change level**.
4. Choose the level. Their current one is marked **Current level**.
5. Select the **Move to** button. It names the level you chose.

Their card updates now. No message or gift is sent. Their stamps, points and rewards stay. They start counting toward the next level from the level you chose.

<OpenInDashboard path="/customers" label="Open Customers" />

<Details summary="Send the level-up message">
When you move a customer up, a box appears: **Send the level-up message and their Silver gift**. Silver stands for your own level's name. Select it to send the message and give the gift. Moving down never sends anything.

A customer gets the message and the gift for a level once. If they already reached that level, the move is quiet.
</Details>

<Details summary="Where level changes show">
On **Activity**, open the **Type** filter and choose **Level changes**. Rows read, for example, "moved up to Silver" or "was moved to Gold". The customer's own history lists the same moves.
</Details>

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="It says &quot;Someone else just updated this customer&quot;">
The customer was scanned at the same moment. Select **Change level** again and repeat the move.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="There is no Change level button">
Levels are off. The button appears only while levels are on, so switch them back on first.
</Symptom>
