The scanner app is how your team adds stamps and points at the counter. It's free, and it's called **Stampeo** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761758382) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hryvnt.stampeo).

## Sign in as the owner or an admin

Use the same account as your dashboard.

1. Open the app and tap **Sign in**.

![The app's first screen, with Join my team and Sign in](/help/install-the-scanner-app-01.png)

2. Tap **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with email**, whichever you use for the dashboard.

![The sign-in choices: Continue with Google, Continue with Apple and Continue with email](/help/install-the-scanner-app-02.png)

3. The first time, the app shows a short tour of how scanning works.

From then on, tap **Start Scanning** whenever a customer shows their card.

The first time you scan, the app asks for your camera. Tap **Continue**, then allow it.

## Join someone's team

You need the six-character code they gave you. Open the app, tap **Join my team** and type the code. See [Add a team member](/help/add-a-team-member#for-the-person-being-invited) for every step.

<Details summary="On an iPad or a tablet at the till">
The app works on iPads and Android tablets, held upright. It scans with the camera on the back, so place the tablet where staff can turn it towards the customer's phone.
</Details>

<Details summary="Keep the app up to date">
Phones usually update apps by themselves, but not always. If the app shows **Update required**, tap **Update now**. To check yourself, search for Stampeo in the App Store or Google Play and tap **Update** if you see it.
</Details>

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="I said no to the camera by mistake">
Tap **Open Settings** on the app's screen, then switch on the camera for Stampeo.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="I forgot my password">
The app can't reset passwords. Reset it on the website: on the [sign-in page](/login), select **Forgot password?**. Then sign in to the app with your new password.
</Symptom>
