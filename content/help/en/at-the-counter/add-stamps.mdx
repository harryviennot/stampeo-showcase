The customer shows their card, you scan it and add the stamp. Their card updates on their phone within a few seconds.

## Add a stamp

1. In the scanner app, tap **Start Scanning**.
2. Point the camera at the QR code on the customer's card. A screenshot of the card works too.
3. Check the customer's name and how many stamps they have, for example **4 / 10**.
4. Tap **Add Stamp**.

The app shows **Stamp Added!**. Tap **Scan Next Customer** for the next person.

## Add several stamps at once

For a group or a bigger order, tap **+** before you add. The button changes to **Add 3 stamps**, or however many you chose, up to 50 at once. When you reach the end of the card, the app shows **This completes the card**.

![The stamp screen with the stepper at 3 and the button reading Add 3 stamps](/help/add-stamps-01.png)

## When the card is full

The app shows **Card Complete!**. Tap **Redeem Reward** to give the reward now, or **Skip for Now** to keep it for their next visit.

If your card saves rewards and keeps going, the app shows **Reward saved!** instead, and the customer starts a new card straight away.

<Details summary="Your card uses points">
Instead of adding a stamp, type the amount the customer spent, then tap **Add Points**. The app works out the points for you.
</Details>

<Details summary="Your shop uses levels">
The customer's level shows next to their name, for example **Silver**. The card shows the size of their level's card, so a Silver card can read **0 / 8** while Bronze reads 0 / 10.

If their level has perks, a box above the card lists them, for example **Silver perk: Free refill**. Apply them before you charge.

When a scan moves the customer up, the app shows the customer's first name and the level, for example **Camille reached Silver!**. A **Tell them** box says what they now get. On a points card, the same appears above the keypad.

A one-time level gift appears under **Rewards to redeem** with a **Gift** tag.
</Details>

## Troubleshooting

<Symptom title="It says &quot;Invalid QR code&quot;">
You've probably scanned your shop's QR code, the one customers use to get the card. Scan the QR code on the customer's own card instead, in their wallet app.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says the card isn't recognised">
The card belongs to another shop, or it's an old copy. Ask the customer to open your card in their wallet app, then scan again.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;Daily limit reached&quot;">
On Growth and Pro, your program can limit what one customer earns in a day or a week. An owner or admin can tap **Add anyway**. A scanner can't, so ask a manager.
</Symptom>

<Symptom title="It says &quot;Slow network, still sending.&quot;">
Wait a moment. The app checks whether the stamp went through and never counts it twice. If it then says **Nothing was recorded. Try again.**, scan the card again.
</Symptom>
